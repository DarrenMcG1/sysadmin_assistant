paragraphs beneath. 83 paragraphs, 38 of them declaring a session and a

**Why it moved, and it is the fold rather than the prose.** The region is
unindented, and `read_entries` ends an entry at the next top-level `- `
bullet — so every line of it was appended to the body of whichever entry
preceded it. On the day of the move that was `SNAG-SYSD-008`, whose 18-line
body read as **247 lines carrying 469 bolded spans**, and a sweep of the
register's bolded figures read 166 where the entries hold 108.

**No shipped consumer minded, and the first draft of this paragraph said
otherwise on a mechanism it had reasoned about rather than driven.** It
claimed the fold was inert by accident of ordering — that
`declared_disposition` takes the *first* `**Status:**` a body declares, so
only the entry's own bullet preceding the region kept it honest. Driven, it
is inert by **construction**: `STATUS_FIELD_RE` requires a *bulleted*
`- **Status:**`, and a synthetic entry declaring none, followed by prose that
spells one, still reads `None`. This region carries **0** bulleted lines, **0**
Status matches and **0** markers — its two `<!--check:` spellings sit inside
code spans, which `strip_code_spans` blanks. Session 194's docstring claim
that *"the dispositions and the markers are found by pattern, and the region
carries neither"* is exactly right, and the correction runs the other way.

**Nothing published moved with it**, measured either side rather than
assumed: estate-manager's `read_snags` reads **144 rows** with the region
present and **144** with it gone, and `read_entries` reads 108 entries at 27
open both ways. What the removal corrects is the corrupted body and nothing
else.

**It is closed to new writing, and the reason it may be is that it was
already a copy.** Its newest paragraph is Session 170's, 24 sittings before
the move. **85 of the 85** `SNAG-*` ids this file names exist as entries in
the register; **88 %** of its distinctive identifiers and figures appear
